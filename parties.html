Deposit & cancellation

We keep it simple and stress-free, because parties should be fun — not stressful.

💛 Deposit

  • A $100 deposit secures your date and slot.
  • The deposit is credited toward your final balance.
  • Balance is due one week before the party.
  • Final headcount confirmed 72 hours ahead.

🌿 Cancellation

  • Cancel 14+ days ahead for a full deposit refund.
  • Within 14 days, the deposit transfers to a new date.
  • One free reschedule per booking.
  • We'll always work with you for weather or illness.

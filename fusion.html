☕ Right next door

Coffee & treats at Fusion

Fusion is the independent little coffee shop right beside Little Town Playhouse. Locally roasted coffee, fresh snacks and proper treats — pop in before, during or after play.

A friendly neighbour, not a concession. Fusion is a separate, independently-run business next to the playhouse — so you get a genuinely great coffee shop on the doorstep, and Little Town members enjoy a standing discount.

Coffee worth the trip

Fusion's beans come from a small roaster just up the road, pulled fresh all day. Decaf, oat milk and little-one-friendly options are always on hand.

It's the perfect partner to a morning of play — caffeinate, refuel, and head back into the town.

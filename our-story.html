How it began

Built by parents, for parents

On a grey winter afternoon, our founder Maya watched her two-year-old turn a pile of wooden blocks into a whole little town — a shop here, a café there, a tiny hospital for a poorly teddy. The living room was a disaster. The kids were in heaven.

There was nowhere nearby that captured that feeling: imaginative, calm, beautifully made, and genuinely welcoming for the adults along for the ride. So she decided to build one.

Little Town Playhouse is that idea, grown up just a little — a whole downtown of storefronts where children try on the roles of everyday life, and parents get a comfy seat and a proper coffee.

"If you give a child the right space, they'll build the whole world — and show you things about it you'd forgotten."

— Maya, Founder of Little Town Playhouse

The journey

How Little Town grew

From a living-room floor to a real little downtown — one block at a time.

A box of blocks

Maya's toddler builds an imaginary town on the living-room floor. The idea is born.

A big idea

Months of sketching storefronts, researching play science, and talking to dozens of local families.

Building the town

Local craftspeople shape every counter and signpost by hand. Every corner gets a name.

Opening day

The doors open on a sunny Saturday. The first child walks straight to the wooden bakery and opens for business.

What we believe

Our vision & values

Three simple ideas guide everything in town.

1

Play is real work

Pretend shopping, caring and serving build empathy, language and confidence. We design every corner to spark it.

2

Made with care

Warm materials, rounded edges and a soft palette — a space that feels handmade, not plastic and loud.

3

Grown-ups matter too

Good coffee, comfy seating and clear sightlines. A visit should recharge the whole family.

Our promise to parents

Safe, supervised, spotless

Safety first

Padded play zones, secure single-point check-in, and equipment inspected daily by our team.

Caring supervision

Friendly, trained play hosts on the floor at all times — and a grown-up always stays with each child.

Always clean

High-touch surfaces sanitised throughout the day, with a full deep-clean every night.

Good to know

Keep it safe & happy

No booking needed — just show up, socks on, play all day. A few simple guidelines so every child has a great time.

Ages & supervision

  • Designed for ages 2–7.
  • This is a watch-your-own-child space — a grown-up stays with their little one and keeps them in sight the whole visit.
  • You know your child best: please always know where they are and step in when they need you.
  • Grown-ups always play free — no limit per child.

Play-floor rules

  • Socks only on the play floor — no shoes for kids or adults.
  • Gentle hands and kind voices — we all share the town.
  • Please keep little ones home if they're feeling unwell.

Food & drink

  • No outside food on the play floor — Fusion next door has you covered.
  • Water bottles are welcome any time, anywhere.
  • Got allergy questions? The team next door at Fusion is happy to help.
Before you come

Play-day FAQ

Quick answers to the things parents ask most before a first visit.

Nope — open play is walk-in any day we're open. Paid online or a member? Just walk straight in. If you'd rather pay in person or have a question, Fusion next door can sort you out. Weekends after lunch are our busiest stretch, so an earlier visit usually means more elbow room.

The town is built for ages 2–7. The storefronts are pitched at preschool and early-elementary imaginations — the littler ones love the hands-on, soft-play corners, and the bigger kids love running the café register and "shopping" with play money.

Water bottles, yes — please. Outside food stays off the play floor, but Fusion next door has kid-friendly snacks, sandwiches and coffee whenever you need a break.

Pop into Fusion next door if you'd like to talk it through with the team. All play surfaces are wiped down throughout the day and we don't use latex anywhere on the floor.

Day passes are valid the day they're issued. If something comes up — sick child, weather, traffic — just let us know and we'll happily transfer your pass to another day. Bought a Household Family Pass? Same deal — just reach out and we'll make it right.

The storefronts

Three shops, endless stories

Each storefront is its own little world — walk up, step in and let the story unfold.

🛒 Shop!

The Grocery

Fill a basket, ring up the register and bag the groceries. Pretend produce, play money and a real-feel checkout for budding shopkeepers.

☕ Serve!

The Corner Café

Take orders, froth the (pretend) milk and deliver treats to tiny tables. A favourite for little baristas and their very willing customers.

🩺 Heal!

The Little Clinic

Bandage a teddy, check a heartbeat and write a get-well note. Gentle role-play that helps little ones feel brave and kind.

Good to know

Keep it safe & happy

A few simple guidelines so every child (and grown-up) has a brilliant time.

Ages & supervision

  • Designed for ages 0–8; the infant nook is gated for under-2s.
  • A grown-up (16+) must stay on-site with each child at all times.
  • One free adult per paid child; additional adults are always free.
  • Children must be signed in and out by their grown-up.

Play-floor rules

  • Grippy socks required for all children and adults on the floor.
  • No outside food or drink — our café next door has you covered.
  • Please keep little ones home if they're feeling unwell.
  • Gentle hands and kind voices — we all share the town.

Our story

Built by parents, for parents

Little Town began at a kitchen table, with a stack of wooden blocks and a wish for somewhere warm to take the kids on a rainy afternoon — a place that felt as good for grown-ups as it did for little ones.

Today that idea is a whole little downtown: friendly, beautifully made, and built around real childhood play.

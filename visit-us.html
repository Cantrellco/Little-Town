Getting here

Plan your visit

Three easy steps to a great day out.

Stop in

No booking needed for open play — paid online or a member? Just show up and walk straight in. Need a pass or a hand? Fusion next door has you covered.

Socks on, shoes off

Everyone on the play floor needs to be in socks — no shoes please. Bring a clean pair for each little one and grown-up.

Play the town!

Explore the market, bakery, fire station and more. Parents relax — Fusion coffee is right next door.

Plan your visit

Three easy steps to a great day out.

Pop in

No booking needed for open play — just turn up, pay at the door (or scan your member QR code) and you're in.

Grippy socks on

Everyone on the play floor needs grippy socks. Forgot yours? No worries — we sell them at the desk.

Play the town!

Explore the market, bakery, fire station and more. Parents relax — Fusion coffee is right next door.
